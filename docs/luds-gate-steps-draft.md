Level flow (major beats):
1. Lair of the Damned — follow the torch gang member along plank
   walkways to the shallow-pools room. The Damned are friendly now —
   do NOT fire weapons here.
2. Left doorway (middle one skips a secret) -> slide, spiked-ceiling
   room: pull up right fast, crouch-pull to avoid damage -> button
   room -> SECRET #1 room (large medi pack, flares) -> climb back.
3. Egyptian Exhibit + offices — pillar/block climbing, switches that
   move doors and ladders, a long shaft; get the EMBALMING FLUID from
   between the sarcophagi (break-away tiles + ramp slides).
4. Back to the Lair (coil blocks as ladders) — SECRET #4 crawlspace
   (rocket + large medi). Place the EMBALMING FLUID in the lighted
   alcove in Geordie Bob's room -> exit opens.
5. Underwater areas — trapdoor shaft into a pool; get the UPV
   (underwater propulsion vehicle). Big cavern: crocodiles; air hole
   in a right-wall opening. Do NOT take the wide brick tunnel (far
   left) until finished here — the current is one-way.
6. Guard + frogman area (secrets #5/#6) — underwater levers/buttons
   open trapdoors/doors; stay unseen: do NOT fire weapons near the
   guard or the alarm closes a door and secret #6 is lost.
7. Large flooded room with diamond-shaped openings — diver + croc;
   air pocket top-left opening on the right wall; crystal + medi pack
   in the lower openings.
